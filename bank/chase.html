How to get the PDF from Chase

  1. Sign in at chase.com and open the account.
  2. Go to Statements, pick the month you need and download the PDF.
  3. Drop that file into the box above.

What a Chase statement looks like inside

This is the part most converters get wrong, so it is worth knowing what happens to your file.

  • Chase checking statements have a TRANSACTION DETAIL section with four columns: date, description, amount and running balance.
  • Dates are written as MM/DD, and the year only appears in the statement header - the converter picks it up from there.
  • Long descriptions wrap onto a second line, and the card number often sits on a line of its own. Those lines belong to the transaction above them, not to a new one.
  • The column positions shift slightly between pages of the same statement - a common reason other converters mix up the amount and the balance.

Chase credit card statements are laid out differently from checking statements - they carry two dates per line (transaction and posting) and no running balance. Both work here.
