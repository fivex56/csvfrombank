How to get the PDF from Bank of America

  1. Sign in at bankofamerica.com and open the account.
  2. Go to Statements and Documents and download the PDF.
  3. Drop that file into the box above.

What a Bank of America statement looks like inside

This is the part most converters get wrong, so it is worth knowing what happens to your file.

  • Transactions are grouped into sections - deposits, withdrawals, checks, fees - each with its own small table.
  • Dates are MM/DD/YY, and descriptions frequently run onto a second line.
  • Section subtotals and the page footers are skipped, so they do not turn into fake transactions in your spreadsheet.
Checked, not claimed: Tested on the official Bank of America sample statement: transaction rows are picked up, and the converter says plainly that there is no running balance to verify against rather than pretending it is sure.

Bank of America can also export CSV directly from online banking for recent periods. If that option is there, use it - it is the bank's own data. This page is for the months where only the PDF is left.
