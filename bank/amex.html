How to get the PDF from American Express

  1. Log in to americanexpress.com and open the card.
  2. Open Statements and Activity and download the PDF.
  3. Drop that file into the box above.

What a American Express statement looks like inside

This is the part most converters get wrong, so it is worth knowing what happens to your file.

  • Card statements have no running balance column, so the converter cannot check itself against one - and it says so on screen instead of quietly guessing.
  • Each line often carries two dates (when the charge happened and when it posted). Only the first goes into your spreadsheet; the second is not treated as part of the description.
  • Payments and credits are printed with a minus sign or in brackets, and both are read correctly.
Checked, not claimed: Tested on the official American Express sample statement: charges, payments and fees come through with the right signs.

Amex statements include a lot of explanatory text around the table. Legal footers and how-to-read blocks are cut out of descriptions.
